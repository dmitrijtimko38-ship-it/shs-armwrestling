FUTURE MATCHUP IDEAS

MATCHUPS TO WATCH ⚡

Seven interesting potential rivalries, based on reported pre-club results and fighting styles. These are predictions, not scheduled matches.

UNOFFICIAL PICKS
01 · HEAVYWEIGHTTITLE REMATCH
Ansh VS Mark

Mark was the former #1 until Ansh defeated him. Can Mark’s adaptable hook/toproll counter Ansh’s explosive start and massive hand in a rematch?

02 · HEAVYWEIGHTNEW CHALLENGE
Lucas VS Illia

Lucas mixes hook and toproll, while Illia attacks fingers and hand position. An intriguing test of versatility versus hand control; no direct result has been reported.

03 · HEAVYWEIGHTRIVALRY
Mark VS Lucas

Lucas previously beat Ansh, but his most recent reported match was a loss to Mark. Another meeting could show whether Lucas has found an answer to Mark’s adaptable style.

04 · MIDDLEWEIGHTCONTENDER TEST
Isaac VS Jaeyoung

Isaac’s dominant hook and side pressure against Jaeyoung’s toproll. Can Jaeyoung keep the match outside, or will Isaac force it into his preferred hook?

05 · CROSS-DIVISION CONCEPTHOOK VS HYBRID
Isaac VS Lucas

Isaac dominated Illia 3–0 despite being smaller, making his hook and side pressure intriguing against Lucas’s adaptable hook/toproll. A hypothetical matchup only, subject to safe weight and age matching.

06 · CROSS-DIVISION CONCEPTFORMER CHAMPION TEST
Isaac VS Mark

Could Isaac’s powerful inside game trouble the former heavyweight #1? Mark’s ability to switch between hook and toproll makes this an interesting tactical comparison, not a prediction of the winner.

08 · OPENWEIGHT DREAM MATCHDAVID VS GOLIATH
Isaac VS Ansh

A smaller, hook-dominant standout versus the explosive heavyweight #1 with an enormous hand. The ultimate theoretical style clash—but a large size gap could make a real school match inappropriate.

For discussion only. No bouts are confirmed. Any future competition requires school approval, appropriate age/weight matching, proper tables, and qualified adult supervision.
